Featured / Market research

Vinyl beyond release week

Vinyl grew while CDs declined. I traced that puzzle from RIAA market totals to a dataset of 132 albums and broader catalog-sales breakdowns. The strongest finding: older recordings account for a larger share of vinyl purchases than CD purchases.
