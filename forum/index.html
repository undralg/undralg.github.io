The approach

Worked on a team-built course platform that connects recording uploads, FFmpeg audio extraction, Whisper transcription and AI-generated summaries, topics and action items. The application uses Flask, React and SQLite, with course membership controlling access.

What the analysis shows

The platform brings recordings, transcripts and notes into one searchable workflow. My contribution focused on the relational database and data-integrity testing. The project includes session-processing states and preserves transcripts when note generation fails.
