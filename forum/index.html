Abstract

Forum is a team-built platform that turns class recordings into searchable transcripts and structured notes. I focused on the relational database and data-integrity testing. The application connects course access, recording uploads, transcription and note generation, with explicit processing states and preservation of transcripts if a later stage fails.

A workflow that can fail in stages

Recording uploads pass through FFmpeg audio extraction, Whisper transcription and AI note generation. Keeping the transcript separate from the generated notes allows a useful intermediate result to survive a downstream failure.
