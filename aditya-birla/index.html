Aditya Birla: allocating capital internationally

Which international investments fit a diversified group’s existing capabilities?

Historical course case study

DCFCapital allocationGlobal finance
Read analysisOpen financial model

The approach

Developed an India–UAE SME payments and working-capital proposal and an UltraTech investment proposal. Built parent-level cash-flow forecasts, ownership adjustments and valuation scenarios.

What the analysis shows

The base models produce positive parent NPVs for both proposals. Their attractiveness depends on execution, volume, terminal value and the group’s ability to apply existing capabilities.

How to read the results

These are proposed investments and assumption-based forecasts, not projects implemented by the company.
