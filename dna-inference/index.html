Inferring family relationships from DNA

How can we assess distant kinship when only limited relatives are available?

Capstone research · Institute of Calculus, University of Buenos Aires

Bayesian inferenceRSimulationGenetics

The approach

Developed an Approximate Bayesian Computation pipeline that simulates DNA-sharing patterns under competing pedigree hypotheses, summarizes shared segments and estimates support for related versus unrelated models. The capstone examines 11 pedigrees and includes repeated cross-validation and label-permutation checks.

What the analysis shows

The framework makes the relationship hypothesis, reference populations and uncertainty explicit. It was motivated by humanitarian identification work in Argentina, where ambiguous genetic evidence has consequences for families.

How to read the results

Internal cross-validation measures performance within the constructed reference banks. It is not a real-world identification accuracy estimate, and should not be directly compared with STR detection power as though the metrics were interchangeable.
