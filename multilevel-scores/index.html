The approach

Modeled latent test-score distributions from grouped observations, comparing pooling choices across years while retaining the information represented by each histogram bin.

What the analysis shows

The analysis tackles a common practical problem: the raw records are unavailable, yet a comparison is still needed. Probabilistic modeling expresses uncertainty instead of pretending the bin midpoints are individual observations.

How to read the results

The conclusions depend on distributional assumptions and the information retained in the published histograms.
