RVD.AI: financing an early-stage venture

How does the size of an initial investment change the ownership bargain?

Historical course case study

Venture valuationTAMDilutionScenario analysis
Read analysisOpen financial model

The approach

Built a market-sizing and revenue scenario, applied a venture-capital valuation method and compared $2.5 million and $3.5 million funding cases. Examined sensitivity to market share and required returns.

What the analysis shows

In the report’s fixed post-money valuation scenario, larger funding sharply increases the investor’s ownership requirement. The case makes the growth-versus-dilution tradeoff explicit.

How to read the results

Market share, exit multiples and future margins are scenario assumptions. This is a historical academic analysis, not a completed financing.
