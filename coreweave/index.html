CoreWeave: valuing capital-intensive AI

How much of a fast-growing infrastructure company’s valuation depends on distant cash flows?

Team course project · Undral Ganbaatar, Shazil Farukh & Anne Behme

DCFValuationComparablesSensitivity analysis
Read analysisOpen financial model

The approach

The team combined a DCF, public comparables and precedent transactions, with sensitivity analysis around financing costs, terminal growth, investment and working capital.

What the analysis shows

The April 2025 report presents a $10-per-share base DCF estimate and a $22 synthesis across methods. The important analytical issue is the spread between methods and the sensitivity to long-run assumptions.

How to read the results

This is a dated team case analysis, not a current price target. Segment-based comparables and terminal-value assumptions require particular care.
