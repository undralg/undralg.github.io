Huzur: AI with hidden information

Can an AI opponent play a traditional Mongolian card game without seeing the other players’ cards?

Independent software project

JavaScriptPythonCloudflareGame AI
Play HuzurView code

The approach

Built a browser game for 2–5 players, with local human and AI seats, multiplayer lobbies and persistent match records. AI decisions combine strategic heuristics, lookahead and exact two-player endgame search. Offline self-play supports policy comparisons.

What the analysis shows

The project connects a playable product with an evaluation loop: log decisions, simulate opponents, compare policies and revise the strategy. Fairness guards keep hidden opponent cards and deck order out of bot decision inputs.

How to read the results

Simulated match results do not establish performance against human players. The documented multiplayer roadmap still includes server-side rules validation.
