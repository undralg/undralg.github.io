Freddo: growth under inflation

How can an expanding business manage cash when inflation and shipping complicate the cycle?

Historical course case study

Working capitalExpansion strategyScenario analysis
Read analysisOpen financial model

The approach

Examined receivables, inventory and payables, then developed operational and expansion scenarios including local production and changes in payment terms.

What the analysis shows

The proposal connects expansion with cash discipline: faster collections and shorter inventory cycles can matter as much as new locations.

How to read the results

Shipping costs, target cash-cycle days and expansion benefits are estimates. They are not verified cost savings or realized outcomes.
