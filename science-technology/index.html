The approach

Examined a plan that accelerates sales growth beyond the historical rate. Reconstructed the cash conversion cycle, working-capital needs and reliance on short-term borrowing.

What the analysis shows

The forecast illustrates how rising accounting income can coexist with cash pressure. Inventory and receivables become central to the credibility of the growth plan.

How to read the results

Forecast improvements in inventory management are assumptions, not observed achievements.
