Toyota: financial policy across borders

How do production location, liquidity and currency exposure shape financial policy?

Historical course case study

Global financeRatio analysisCurrency exposure
Read analysisOpen financial model

The approach

Compared Toyota’s financial ratios with automaker peers, then examined domestic production, international competitiveness and financial policy in the case’s 2010–2011 setting.

What the analysis shows

The case connects balance-sheet decisions to operating geography. A consolidated ratio alone can obscure the different funding needs of manufacturing and financial services.

How to read the results

Historical peer comparisons depend on consistent segment definitions. The case is not a statement about Toyota’s current financial position.
