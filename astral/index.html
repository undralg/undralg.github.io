Astral Records: growth needs financing

Can a growing company fund expansion while margins weaken?

Historical course case study

The approach

Reviewed historical profitability and leverage, then forecast 1994–1995 financial statements with and without capital expenditure. Connected operating performance to financing requirements.

What the analysis shows

Revenue growth alone does not answer the funding question. The case brings margin pressure, debt servicing and capital spending into the same forecast.

How to read the results

Historical classroom case; projections depend on the case inputs and operating assumptions.
