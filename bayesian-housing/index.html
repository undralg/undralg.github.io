The approach

Compared Normal, Student-t and outlier-aware Bayesian regression specifications for Buenos Aires property pricing.

What the analysis shows

Treating the likelihood as a modeling decision makes the influence of unusual observations visible. The report compares specifications rather than relying on a single error model.

How to read the results

Results describe the project dataset and modeling assumptions; they are not a deployed appraisal system.
